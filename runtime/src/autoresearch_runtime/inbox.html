New here? How to read results and steer

Start here explains the project's goal and why each effort was tried. A study report gives its result and the evidence used to make a decision.

Evidence validity means the stated checks passed; it is not proof of the hypothesis or permission to deploy. Diagnostic means useful but limited evidence. A failed result can still teach us what to try next.

Comment suggests a direction. Approve authorizes only a named eligible study action at this exact report revision. Pause takes effect on that study at its next safe complete-unit checkpoint. It does not terminate a running experiment mid-unit. Overview comments never grant these permissions.

Glossaries explain domain terms. Metrics state units, number of observations and acceptance rules. Expand Source reports for immutable evidence; publication times tell you how fresh the summary is.

Source evidence and feedback remain in their original language; translations change presentation only.
